Color chase sweeps colors, then lands on the last color you picked in Celica Glow. Keep my color never changes the color: the zones light up one at a time and fade in, so it keeps whatever the controller already had, even a color you set in the GuanQi app.

On: colors chase between Zone 1 and Zone 2 while brightness builds, then everything fills to your color. Off: a short chase, then the zones shut off one at a time. The phone runs this, so Celica Glow has to be open and connected.
